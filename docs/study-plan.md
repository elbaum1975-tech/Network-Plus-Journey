# Network+ eight-week study plan

This is a flexible plan for a learner studying toward CompTIA Network+. It is independent study guidance, not an official course syllabus. Match it to the current exam objectives and your instructor’s schedule. The week-by-week Messer section references below are based on Professor Messer’s N10-009 course; confirm N10-009 is the right exam version before using them.

## A steady weekly routine

Plan for five short sessions each week. Adjust the time to your own schedule and coursework:

1. **Learn:** Watch a small set of lessons and read the matching course material. Explain each concept in your own words.
2. **Recall:** Review flashcards, ports, protocols, diagrams, and subnetting without looking at notes.
3. **Apply:** Complete a small lab or draw a configuration. Write down what you expected and what happened.
4. **Practice:** Answer exam-style questions. Mark both incorrect answers and lucky guesses.
5. **Repair:** Revisit weak concepts, answer similar questions again, and plan the next week.

Use a study log to track the lesson sections completed, questions attempted, weak objectives, and next action. Do not count video time alone as mastery: practice explaining and applying each concept.

## Week-by-week focus

| Week | Focus | Messer course sections | Example practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Networking concepts: OSI, devices, cloud, ports, and protocols | 1.1–1.4 | Explain a DNS lookup and a TCP connection; identify common ports from memory. |
| 2 | Cabling, transceivers, topologies, IPv4, IPv6, and network environments | 1.5–1.8 | Compare media and connectors; do subnetting drills; label a small network diagram. |
| 3 | Network implementation: routing, switching, wireless, physical installation | 2.1–2.4 | Draw an addressed LAN; explain VLANs, routes, Wi-Fi security, and switch loops. |
| 4 | Network operations: documentation, monitoring, services, and access | 3.1–3.5 | Read sample command output and logs; document a change; compare DNS, DHCP, and NTP. |
| 5 | Network security concepts and controls | 4.1–4.3 | Match threats to controls; explain authentication, firewall rules, and secure wireless. |
| 6 | Troubleshooting methodology and common network faults | 5.1–5.5 | Diagnose one fault at a time; record symptoms, evidence, cause, fix, and verification. |
| 7 | Mixed review and performance-based practice | Revisit weakest sections | Work through timed mixed questions and configuration or command-output scenarios. |
| 8 | Final review and readiness | Revisit weak objectives and recent study-group questions | Take a timed practice exam, review every miss, and discuss readiness with your instructor. |

## How to know what to study next

Use the current CompTIA objectives as the source of truth. For each objective, mark whether you can:

- recognize the terms;
- explain the idea without notes;
- apply it to a scenario or command output;
- perform it in an authorized lab and explain the result.

Spend the next study session on the weakest high-impact objective. If you fall behind, continue from the next unfinished topic instead of restarting the entire plan.

## Readiness check

Before booking an exam, confirm the exam version with your instructor, use the matching official objectives, explain each objective in your own words, and complete multiple timed practice sets with review of all missed and guessed questions. Practice results are useful signals, not a guarantee of passing.
